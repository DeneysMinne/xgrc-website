1Executive summary

The XGRC® platform holds the records organisations are audited on: risks, incidents, controls, obligations, suppliers and evidence. We protect it accordingly, under an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified information security management system.

Strategix Application Solutions (Pty) Ltd is the original developer and operator of XGRC®. More than 800 companies on six continents use it. That gives us one responsibility above all others: the information you put into XGRC® stays confidential, intact and available, and remains yours.

This document explains how we meet that responsibility, from governance and hosting to encryption, access, monitoring, incident response, recovery, suppliers and AI. Where a commitment is contractual, we name the document that carries it: the XGRC® SaaS Agreement, the Data Processing Addendum, the Support Policy and the Cybersecurity & Data Protection Policy, all published in our Trust Centre at xgrcsoftware.com/trust.

We also run our own information security management system on XGRC® itself. The policies, risks, controls, vulnerabilities and audits described here are recorded, assessed and tracked in the same platform our customers use.

2Governance and certification

Our information security management system (ISMS) is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

Its scope covers the design, development, delivery and support of the XGRC® cloud-based platform. BSI issued the certificate, IS 804839, on 29 August 2024; it is valid until 28 August 2027, and anyone can check it in BSI's public certificate directory.

How the ISMS works

  • Policies: a documented Information Security Policy and supporting policies for access control, cryptography, asset management, supplier security, communications, physical security, business continuity, incident management and secure development (Appendix A lists them).
  • Risk management: information security risks are assessed and treated through a documented process, with a Statement of Applicability against the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls and a maintained risk treatment plan.
  • Roles: defined roles, responsibilities and authorities for information security, with management accountable for the ISMS.
  • Measurement: security objectives, a monitoring and measurement plan, an internal audit programme and management reviews.
  • Improvement: nonconformities and corrective actions are logged and closed through a continual improvement process.
  • External audit: an independent certification body audits the ISMS every year, with a full recertification audit every three years.

Frameworks we align to

Alongside ISO/IEC 27001:2022, our security practices draw on the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (identify, protect, detect, respond, recover), the CIS Critical Security Controls and the OWASP Top 10 for web application risks.

Privacy law

We operate under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and, where it applies to our customers, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Section 4 explains how.

Compliance monitoring

Compliance is monitored continuously, both for our own ISMS and for the obligations our customers track in XGRC®. Regulatory content and platform controls are updated as laws and standards change, so customers stay ahead of new requirements and reduce the risk of fines and penalties.

3Hosting, architecture and data residency

XGRC® runs on Microsoft Azure in the West Europe region (Netherlands), behind layered network defences, with every customer's data logically isolated from every other.

Where your data lives

  • Primary hosting: Microsoft Azure, West Europe (Netherlands). Customer Data is stored there.
  • Microsoft's own assurance: Azure's infrastructure holds ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27017, ISO/IEC 27018 and SOC 2 Type II certifications, and Microsoft runs the physical data centre security.
  • Subprocessors: Microsoft Azure (hosting, infrastructure, storage) and Microsoft 365 (email delivery and notifications), both in West Europe. The live list is at xgrcsoftware.com/trust/subprocessors. Hakware, our security operations partner, sees only technical security telemetry, so it is not a subprocessor.
  • Cross-border transfers: where personal information leaves South Africa, it is protected by the safeguards in the Data Processing Addendum and applicable law.

How the platform is built

  • Edge: Cloudflare DNS and web application firewall filter traffic and block common web attacks before they reach Azure.
  • Application and API tiers: separate application and API sets, each behind its own web application firewall.
  • Load balancing: Azure Load Balancer spreads traffic across servers for availability.
  • Network segregation: network security groups restrict traffic between tiers to what each needs.
  • Data tier: Azure SQL Managed Instance, reachable only from the application tiers.
  • Identity: Microsoft Entra ID for authentication and single sign-on.
  • Scaling: resources scale automatically under heavy load, so peaks do not degrade service.

Multi-tenancy

XGRC® is a multi-tenant platform with strict logical data isolation between customers, so one customer cannot see another's data.

5Encryption and key management

Your data is encrypted whenever it moves and wherever it is stored.

  • In transit: all traffic between users, the platform and its services uses TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3. Plain HTTP is redirected to HTTPS, and browsers are instructed to use HTTPS only (HTTP Strict Transport Security).
  • At rest: stored data is encrypted with AES-256, the 256-bit Rijndael standard.
  • Per-customer keys: each customer's data is encrypted with keys unique to that customer, so one customer's key cannot open another's data.
  • Key management: keys are generated, stored, rotated and retired under our Cryptography Policy, with access limited to authorised personnel.
  • Mobile: data the SHEQX® mobile apps capture offline is encrypted on the device, and users can clear it from the device at any time. The app keeps its reference data after syncing, so it keeps working offline without downloading everything again.

6Identity and access management

Your administrators decide exactly who sees what, down to a single record; our own staff get only the access their role needs.

For your users

  • Single sign-on: users can sign in through Microsoft Entra ID (Azure Active Directory), so your own password, MFA and leaver policies apply. Microsoft Entra ID is the supported identity provider for SSO.
  • Multi-factor authentication: applied to your users through Microsoft Entra ID, under your own MFA policies.
  • Granular permissions: administrators set add, edit, view and delete rights per module, dashboard and record.
  • Record-level security: access can be limited by business unit and category, so people see only the records that belong to them.
  • Least access by default: newly released features and modules start with no access until your administrator grants it, so an update never widens access silently.
  • Audit trail: changes to records are logged with who made them and when.

For Strategix staff

  • Access to production systems follows least privilege and is granted per role under our Access Control Policy.
  • Multi-factor authentication is required for all administrative access.
  • Access is reviewed regularly and removed promptly when people change role or leave.

7Application security, vulnerabilities and change control

We look for weaknesses every day and close the serious ones immediately, outside the normal release cycle.

  • Secure development: XGRC® is developed under our System Acquisition, Development and Maintenance Policy, designed against the OWASP Top 10 web application risks.
  • Change control: every platform change follows a documented change control process.
  • Vulnerability scanning: automated, AI-driven vulnerability assessments run daily through Hakware, our penetration-testing and vulnerability partner.
  • Tracked to closure: each finding is recorded in XGRC® as a risk, assessed, given an action plan and closed with a control, the same way our customers manage theirs.
  • Penetration testing: automated penetration testing runs daily, and a manual penetration test is carried out every year; an executive summary is available under NDA.
  • Patching: critical security patches are deployed immediately, outside regular update cycles, with little or no disruption to service.
  • Browser protections: the platform sends security headers that block clickjacking and content-type sniffing, alongside HTTPS-only enforcement.

8Security monitoring and operations

The platform is watched around the clock, so threats are found and contained quickly.

  • Security operations centre: 24/7 monitoring by Hakware's security operations centre, using real-time threat intelligence.
  • SIEM: security events from across the environment are collected and correlated in real time.
  • SOAR: security orchestration, automation and response speeds up detection and containment.
  • Logging: security, access and audit events are logged and retained indefinitely, so an investigation can always go back to the start.
  • Server hardening: servers are hardened, with segregated system layers and restricted access to critical functions.
  • Capacity and performance: resources and performance are monitored continuously, with automatic scaling under load.

12AI governance: MAIA® and AI agents

AI in XGRC® is governed like any other part of the platform: every interaction is recorded, every agent is limited to what it is allowed to do, and people make the decisions.

  • Audit trail: MAIA®, the platform's governed AI for business intelligence, data views and intelligent agents, records a full audit trail of AI interactions.
  • Your data and AI models: Strategix never uses customer data to train AI models. You choose which AI model MAIA® uses: with your chosen provider, that provider's own data terms apply, and we can help you run a local model so prompts and data stay within your own environment.
  • Agent credentials: Asgard, the XGRC® AI agents platform, keeps upstream API keys and OAuth tokens in an encrypted vault. Agents receive their own scoped keys, tool calls are brokered and filtered, keys can be revoked centrally, and every agent action is logged.
  • People decide: XLOGIC®, which turns policies and obligations into workflows, lets AI draft proposals but never approve them; every proposal waits for a person.
  • Governing your own AI: customers can govern their own AI systems in XGRC® AI Governance, aligned to ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act.
