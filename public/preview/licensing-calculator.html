Notes
  • ENVIRX, ERM, Integrated Assurance, HACCP and BOWTIE each have their own seat count; it does not need to match your Core count, but cannot exceed it.
  • Selecting Integrated Assurance auto-includes ENVIRX and ERM at your full Core seat count; without it, ENVIRX/ERM can be sized independently.
  • Every licence type, including Lite, Task User and Team User, also includes a standard baseline: Documents, Help Centre, Support, Broadcasting, Incident Quick Log, SBO, Near Miss Quick Log and Employee Portal.
  • Team User is an estimated total employee headcount, not a quantity chosen freely. The product reconciles it against employees already licensed under another seat type once provisioned, so nobody is billed twice. Employee records themselves cost nothing; only enabling their access is billed.
  • MAIA requires 1 or more Full Users, with no upper limit; Data User and Data Used are optional on top of that. Data Used is the one usage-based line item in this quote; every other line is a fixed commitment regardless of actual use.
  • Compliance Hub is a flat monthly fee, not a per-seat cost, but still moves your quote to XGRC Plus.
  • MAIA, Hakware, XLOGIC and Asgard seat counts are independent of your Core count; they represent separate user bases, not a subset of your Core seats.
  • Volume discount thresholds are set independently per currency, not converted from ZAR, the same way list prices are.
  • Annual commitment applies a further 10% to the already-discounted monthly value, compounding.
  • Prices are real per-currency list prices from the XGRC price book, except Admin, XLOGIC and Asgard, which are new/unpriced placeholders.
  • Billing terms: licences are billed monthly in arrears and are payable whether or not a seat was used that month. Annual commitment is invoiced as a single amount, paid in advance.
