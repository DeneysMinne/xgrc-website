Security & Trust
ISO 27001:2022 Certified Hosted on Microsoft Azure, West Europe (Netherlands) · ISO 42001 certification targeted October 2026
Platform security
  • Role-based access control, least privilege and multi-factor authentication for administrative access.
  • Multi-tenant architecture with strict logical data isolation between customers.
  • Data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest using industry-standard algorithms.
Monitoring & resilience
  • Security event monitoring, audit logging and alerting.
  • Regular vulnerability scanning, patch management and penetration testing.
  • Documented business continuity and disaster recovery, with tested backup procedures.
Data protection & assurance
  • Data Processing Addendum satisfying POPIA section 21, with a 72-hour security incident notification commitment.
  • Primary subprocessors are Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365; customers get advance notice of any material change.
  • Certification evidence and audit summaries available on reasonable written request.
Notes
Pricing & seats
  • ENVIRX®, ERM and Integrated Assurance each have their own seat count; it does not need to match your Core and Admin count combined, but cannot exceed it. Admin already has full Core capability, so both count toward the pool.
  • MAIA® and Hakware seat counts are independent of your Core and Admin count; they represent separate user bases, not a subset of those seats.
  • Team User is an estimated total employee headcount, not a quantity chosen freely. The product reconciles it against employees already licensed under another seat type once provisioned, so nobody is billed twice. Employee records themselves cost nothing; only enabling their access is billed.
  • Volume discount thresholds are set independently per currency, not converted from ZAR, the same way list prices are.
  • Annual commitment applies a further 10% to the already-discounted monthly value, compounding.
What's included
  • HACCP and BOWTIE are included discipline/risk methodologies at no extra cost, not seat-based selections.
  • Every licence type, including Lite, Task User and Team User, also includes a standard baseline: Documents, Help Centre, Support, Broadcasting, Incident Quick Log, SBO, Near Miss Quick Log and Employee Portal.
  • MAIA® is switched on with a single tickbox, which opens up its user fields; a minimum of 1 Full User and 1 Data Bucket (50GB) applies while it's enabled, with no upper limit. Data User seats and extra Data Buckets are optional on top of that. Data Buckets is the one usage-based line item in this quote; every other line is a fixed commitment regardless of actual use.
  • Compliance Hub is a flat monthly fee, not a per-seat cost, but still moves your quote to XGRC® Plus.
  • XLOGIC® Viewer is not a paid seat: it's included at no extra cost for Core and Admin users once you're on XGRC® Plus or Enterprise. Provisioning is manual today, pending a direct login link between XGRC® and XLOGIC®.
Pricing status & billing terms
  • XLOGIC® is priced per seat (Admin and Governance User), like every other Enterprise product. AI Governance and Asgard remain flat, unpriced placeholders for now.
  • Prices are real per-currency list prices from the XGRC® price book, except Admin, XLOGIC® Admin and XLOGIC® Governance User, which are new placeholders pending confirmation; AI Governance and Asgard remain entirely unpriced.
  • Billing terms: licences are billed monthly in arrears and are payable whether or not a seat was used that month. Annual commitment is invoiced as a single amount, paid in advance.
