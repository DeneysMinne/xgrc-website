Notes for the rep
· Every module and add-on has its own seat count; it does not need to match your Core count.
· Selecting Integrated Assurance auto-includes ENVIRX and ERM at your full Core seat count; without it, ENVIRX/ERM can be sized independently.
· Task User includes both Action and Dashboard access, combined.
· Every license type, including Lite, Task User and Team User, also includes a standard baseline: Documents, Help Centre, Support, Broadcasting, Incident Quick Log, SBO, Near Miss Quick Log and Employee Portal.
· Team User is not a quantity you choose freely; it is every employee record without another license, once employee access is switched on. Employee records themselves cost nothing; only enabling their access is billed.
· MAIA requires 1 or more Full Users, with no upper limit; Data User and Data Used are optional on top of that.
· Compliance Hub is free, but selecting it still moves the quote to XGRC Plus.
· Volume discount is driven by total monthly list value in ZAR, not seat count.
· Annual commitment applies a further 10% to the already-discounted monthly value, compounding.
· Prices are real per-currency list prices from the XGRC price book, except Admin, XLOGIC and Asgard, which are new/unpriced placeholders.
· ENVIRX/ERM ZAR price uses the corrected R139 figure; the foreign-currency figures have not yet been re-confirmed against that correction.
