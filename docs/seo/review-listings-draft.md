## 2. SHEQX® (safety, health, environment and quality)

**Product name:** SHEQX® by XGRC® Software

**Website:** https://xgrcsoftware.com/sheqx/

> SHEQ software that closes actions, sustains ISO certification and proves it.

(76 characters. Alternative, closer to the site headline: "SHEQ compliance that closes actions, not just spreadsheets.", 58 characters.)

### Short description (about 150 words)

SHEQX® is SHEQ management software from XGRC® Software. It brings safety, health, environment and quality data onto one auditable platform, giving organisations the visibility to prevent incidents, close non-conformances and sustain ISO certification without the administrative drag.

SHEQX® manages the complete SHEQ lifecycle, from hazard identification and incident capture through risk treatment, legal compliance, permits, inspections and ISO-aligned audit management, across every site and business unit. Its 24 modules cover incident management, risk management, emergency preparedness, PPE, medical surveillance, legal requirements and appointments, permits, MSDS management, non-conformances, calibration, training records, audits, inspections, document control and action management.

SHEQX® is aligned to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001, and supports South African obligations under the OHS Act and the Mine Health and Safety Act. Because it shares one data foundation with the wider XGRC® platform, SHEQ risks stay connected to enterprise risk, environmental monitoring and assurance.

### Long description (about 400 words)

SHEQX® is the Safety, Health, Environment and Quality solution within XGRC® Software. It is built for organisations whose incidents, non-conformances, legal obligations and ISO audit evidence are still scattered across email, spreadsheets and paper forms, and whose leadership needs one auditable view of SHEQ performance across multiple sites.

SHEQX® manages the complete SHEQ lifecycle on one platform. Incidents are captured as they happen, with photo and document evidence, classified by severity, investigated through structured root cause analysis and driven through corrective actions to verified closure. Hazards and risks are linked to the controls that treat them. Legal requirements, legal appointments, permits and safety data sheets are held as owned records with expiry tracking, so they do not lapse unnoticed between audits. Audits and inspections are aligned to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001, so certification evidence accumulates as work is done rather than being reassembled before each surveillance visit.

Its 24 modules are organised into five groups:

- **Risk and safety:** incident management, risk management, emergency preparedness, PPE management, medical surveillance and occupational job profiles
- **Compliance and legal:** legal requirements, legal appointments, permit management and MSDS management
- **Quality and operations:** non-conformance management, change management, calibration, objectives and targets, and strategy management
- **People and training:** training records, employee management, stakeholder engagement and suggestions
- **Oversight:** audit management, inspection management, document manager, meeting management and action manager

For multi-site organisations, SHEQX® consolidates SHEQ performance across every site and business unit into real-time dashboards, so group SHEQ managers can see incidents, non-conformances and audit findings across the organisation from one place.

SHEQX® can run on its own or alongside other XGRC® solutions. It shares one data foundation with ENVIRX® for environmental monitoring, Enterprise Risk Management and XGRC® Compliance Hub for contractor compliance, and can be coordinated with other disciplines through MSX®.

Customers include Servest, which runs SHEQX® with ENVIRX® and achieved renewed ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certifications in October 2024; Interwaste, which unified SHEQ processes across multiple regions and increased near-miss reporting volumes; Sandton Plant Hire; Commercial Cold Holdings; and Vican Manufacturing. SHEQX® launched in 2016 and runs on the XGRC® platform, which is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and hosted on Microsoft Azure.

Send to **all active customer contacts** (Deneys' decision), not a hand-picked few, in line with both sites' rules on selective solicitation.
