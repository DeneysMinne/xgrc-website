## Competitor teardown — VelocityEHS `/solution/environmental-compliance/` (#1)

~2,200–2,400 words on a dedicated exact-match solution page; sub-topic sections (air, water,
waste); real FAQ block; regulatory authority signals (ISO 14001, Title V, NPDES, RCRA); repeated
demo CTAs. Our gap: exact-phrase title targeting, on-page proof, and the ISO 27001/BSI trust
signal (which our product pages did not previously show).

## What we're changing (items 1–3)

1. **ENVIRX + SHEQX product pages:** exact-phrase metaTitles; real customer testimonials
   (Servest, Interwaste, Sandton Plant Hire, Commercial Cold Holdings) surfaced per-product;
   ISO 27001:2022-certified-by-BSI trust bar. No invented claims.
2. **Geo-qualified landing pages:** environmental compliance / SHEQ software South Africa,
   mining ESG compliance Africa. Weak competition, we already rank ~12–21.
3. **`/grc-software`:** already exists and is strong — make it (not the homepage) the ranking
   page via canonical targeting + internal linking. Long game on the head term.
